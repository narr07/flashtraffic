# Quickstart Guide

Get up and running with your first FlashTraffic simulation in under 3 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Create a Campaign

1. Open **Campaign Studio** from the sidebar or click **"New Campaign"** in the top navbar.
2. Enter a **Campaign Name** (e.g. *Q4 Landing Page Test*).
3. Set your **Target Sessions Quota** (e.g. *50*).
4. Add target landing page URLs manually or click **"Crawl More URLs"** to discover pages with the built-in link crawler.

---

## Step 2: Choose Traffic Channel & Attribution

Choose how incoming traffic should be attributed:

- **Organic Search**: Select search engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo, Yandex, Baidu, DuckDuckGo) and add target keywords. FlashTraffic will calculate a combinatorial matrix distribution.
- **Social & Referral**: Select target social platforms (Facebook, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, Reddit) or enter a custom referral blog URL.
- **Direct Navigation**: Clean direct address bar navigation without referrer headers.

---

## Step 3: Configure Browser Behavior

Navigate to the **Playwright Engine** tab to adjust simulation physics:

- **Presets**: Choose **"Ultra-Fast"** (500ms exit, 0ms delay, no scroll) for maximum load throughput, or **"Realistic Human"** (4-8s dwell, natural Bézier curves, internal link surfing).
- **Headless Mode**: Run silently in the background or disable headless mode to watch the browser interact with pages live.

---

## Step 4: (Optional) Add Proxies

If you want to simulate global geographic origins:

1. Go to **Proxy Orchestrator**.
2. Click **"URL / Text File"** and click **"Fetch & Extract"** from free sources, or paste your private IP:Port lists.
3. Click **"Test All Latencies"** and **"Prune Dead"**.
4. Toggle **"Enable Proxy"** and click **"Set Alive to Campaign"**.

---

## Step 5: Launch & Monitor

Return to the **Dashboard** and click **"Start Campaign"**.
Watch live concurrent visitors, success rate, and real-time execution logs in the interactive console!
